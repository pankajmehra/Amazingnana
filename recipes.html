A Nana kitchen rule

Let them help, even if it takes longer.

The flour on the counter and the uneven cookies are often the part everybody remembers. Give grandkids a real job and let “good enough” be good enough.

Build a tradition

One recipe can become a family memory.

Choose something simple enough to repeat. Repetition is what turns a recipe into “the thing we always make at Nana’s.”

1

Pick one easy favorite

Cookies, pancakes, banana bread or another recipe everyone genuinely likes.

2

Give each grandchild a job

Measuring, mixing, setting the timer, decorating or writing the date on the recipe card.

3

Write down the story

Add who made it, when you make it and any funny changes your family has made over time.
