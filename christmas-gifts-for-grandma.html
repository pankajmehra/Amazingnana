15 Christmas gifts for Grandma she’ll actually enjoy

The easiest way to choose a good gift is to start with how Grandma spends her time: family photos, reading, cooking, gardening, relaxing at home or telling family stories.

The gifts that are really about time together

Best homemade gift

13. A “date with Grandma” coupon booklet

Write specific coupons for baking cookies, a bookstore visit, lunch together, a walk, movie night or helping with a project. Specific plans are more likely to happen.

Best for a whole family

14. A planned family photo day

Give the date, location and plan as the gift. The finished photos become a second gift later.

Best tradition starter

15. A game plus a promise to play it together

Choose a game that fits the grandkids and make the first game night part of the present. See our game guide for Grandma’s house.

Make the gift easier, not harder.

For anything digital, assemble it, charge it and set it up. For photo gifts, add the photos. For journals, write the first note. Finishing the setup is often what turns a good product into a genuinely thoughtful gift.
