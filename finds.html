Grandma’s house checklist

What is actually useful to keep on hand?

You do not need a room full of toys. A few repeat-use items can cover playtime, meals, quiet time and sleepovers.

🧺

Activity basket

Keep one game, crayons, stickers, paper and a small puzzle together so you always have an easy indoor option.

📚

Books that stay at Nana’s

Let each grandchild choose one favorite book that lives at your house and becomes part of the visit routine.

🏡

Comfort basics

A washable blanket, kid-friendly cup, night-light and a few approved snacks can make visits much easier.
