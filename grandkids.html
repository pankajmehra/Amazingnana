For the grandkids

Make visits feel special.

A growing collection of activities, games and gift ideas for the time grandmas and grandkids get to spend together.

🎲

Game basket

Keep a small basket of 2–3 easy games that only come out at Grandma’s house. It turns ordinary visits into a little tradition.

📚

Story shelf

Let each grandchild pick one book that “lives at Nana’s.” Re-reading favorites can become part of every visit.

🍪

Mini baking day

Choose one easy recipe and let the grandkids measure, stir and decorate. The mess is usually part of the memory.

Featured activity: Zingo

If you have younger grandkids, a simple matching-style game can be an easy way to get everyone around the table without screens.
