For the grandkids

Make visits feel like Grandma’s house.

You don’t need a packed schedule. A few tiny traditions can make ordinary afternoons feel special and give grandkids something to look forward to every time they visit.

Small traditions

Easy ideas they’ll remember.

Low-pressure, repeatable ideas are often better than elaborate plans. The point is the time together.

🎲

The Grandma game basket

Keep 2–3 easy games in one basket that only come out at Grandma’s house. Familiarity turns it into a tradition.

📚

The Nana story shelf

Let each grandchild choose one book that “lives at Nana’s.” Re-reading favorites can become part of every visit.

🍪

One special recipe

Pick one easy cookie, pancake or snack recipe and make it together often enough that it becomes “your thing.”
