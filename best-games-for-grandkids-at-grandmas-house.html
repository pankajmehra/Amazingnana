If you only buy three:

Choose one game for the youngest grandchild, one that the whole family can play, and one very quick game for short visits. That gives you variety without filling a closet.

How to choose a game for Grandma’s house

  • Favor games that can be explained in a few minutes.
  • Keep round length short enough that stopping for dinner is not a crisis.
  • Choose durable pieces and boxes that are easy to store.
  • Match the game to the youngest regular player, not the oldest.
  • Keep one familiar favorite and rotate in a new game occasionally.
