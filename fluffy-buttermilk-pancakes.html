Fluffy Buttermilk Pancakes

A reliable stack of soft, fluffy pancakes for slow weekend mornings, sleepovers and “breakfast at Nana’s.”

10 pancakes25 minutesOne-bowl friendly

Ingredients

10 minPrep
15 minCook
MediumHeat
10Pancakes
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 2 large eggs
  • ¼ cup unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
Little-helper job: let grandkids whisk the dry ingredients and choose toppings.

How to make them

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt.
  2. In a second bowl, whisk the buttermilk, eggs, melted butter and vanilla.
  3. Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients. Stir gently just until combined. A few small lumps are good — overmixing can make pancakes tough.
  4. Heat a skillet or griddle over medium heat and lightly butter it.
  5. Pour about ¼ cup batter for each pancake.
  6. Cook until bubbles appear on top and the edges begin to look set, about 2–3 minutes.
  7. Flip and cook another 1–2 minutes, until golden and cooked through.
  8. Serve warm with fruit, maple syrup, yogurt or a little powdered sugar.

Nana’s make-ahead tip

Cook the whole batch, cool completely and refrigerate for up to 3 days. Reheat in a toaster or warm skillet for an easy breakfast when the grandkids wake up hungry.
