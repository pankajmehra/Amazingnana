From a shirt shop to a community of 59K+.

Amazing Nana started with grandmother-themed products. Over time, the Facebook page became something more valuable: a lively community built around family, humor, pride and the everyday joy of being Grandma.

Why rebuild now

The audience stayed. The old store didn’t need to.

Rather than paying for a full e-commerce platform before it’s necessary, AmazingNana.com is being rebuilt as a lightweight content and recommendation site. That keeps costs low while giving the community useful reasons to visit again.

The idea

Helpful first. Monetization second.

Affiliate recommendations, gift guides and future products should feel like a natural extension of the community — not the reason the community exists.

What we’re building

A better Amazing Nana.

The new site is designed around four simple promises.

💬

Community-led

Facebook conversations help show which topics genuinely resonate with grandmas and families.

🛍️

Curated finds

A few relevant products and guides are more useful than an endless wall of affiliate links.

🍪

Family content

Activities, recipes and traditions give people a reason to visit even when they aren’t shopping.

🤍

Transparent

Affiliate relationships are disclosed clearly, and we won’t claim personal experience we don’t have.
